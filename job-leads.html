What "verified" actually means

Most job alerts are scraped lists full of expired posts. Ours are checked before they ever reach you, every single week.

  • Every posting opened and read — never sent from a snippet or a search result alone.
  • Confirmed live and accepting applications on the morning we send it.
  • Fresh postings — openings posted within the last 7 days, not month-old reposts.
  • Direct links to the employer's posting — no blind "apply here" links, no bait-and-switch.
  • Employer and location confirmed — you always know who you'd be applying to, and where.

Powered by a real employer database

Your leads don't come from a scraped list. They come from our database of 385 verified Georgia employers — hiring contacts and openings re-checked on a regular cycle — matched to what you tell us you want.

Questions, answered

When does it launch, and what does it cost?

We're finalizing payments now and launching soon. Planned pricing is $15/month or $99/year, confirmed at launch before anyone pays. Join the interest list and you'll be notified first — there's no charge or commitment to join.

How are the leads verified?

Every posting is opened and read — never copied from a search snippet. We confirm the employer's own posting is live and accepting applications on the morning your email goes out, that it was posted within the last 7 days, and that the link goes straight to the real application. If we can't confirm a posting, it doesn't go in your email.

How do I receive my leads?

By email, every Monday morning. Each lead shows the job title, employer, location, pay (when the posting lists it), a one-line why-it-fits note, and a direct link to apply.

What areas do you cover?

We're launching with Georgia and remote-US roles. Tell us your metro area when you join the interest list — if we can't cover it well yet, we'll tell you honestly before your first payment.

What if a link is dead when I click it?

Every link is re-verified the morning we send. If one dies mid-week anyway, just reply to the email and we'll replace it in the next batch.

Can I change what I'm looking for?

Yes — reply to any weekly email with your updated preferences and the next Monday's batch will reflect them.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime with one click through the payment customer portal (linked in every email). You keep receiving leads through the end of your billing period. No calls, no retention scripts.

Is this connected to Ticket to Work?

Prestige Path Consulting LLC is an American Dream Employment Network affiliate serving Ticket to Work beneficiaries. The leads service is open to everyone — participants and the general public alike — and subscribing is separate from any Ticket to Work services.
