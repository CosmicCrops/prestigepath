Know Your Numbers

The 2027 amounts

These are the figures the current editions work with. Social Security adjusts most of them most years — when SSA publishes a new set, this page changes and the books stay right.

Number2027 amountWhat it means
Trial Work Period (TWP) — SSDI $1,270 / month A month counts as one of your nine trial work months when your gross earnings top this amount (or you work more than 80 hours in self-employment).
Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA) — non-blind $1,770 / month The earnings level Social Security uses when deciding whether work counts as substantial, for SSDI and SSI work decisions.
Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA) — blind $2,950 / month The SGA level for people who are blind.
SSI federal benefit rate — individual $1,029 / month The maximum federal SSI payment for an individual. Some states add a supplement on top.
SSI federal benefit rate — couple $1,544 / month The maximum federal SSI payment for an eligible couple.
Student Earned Income Exclusion (SEIE) $2,500 / month · $10,080 / year For students under 22 who regularly attend school: earnings SSA excludes before any other math — up to these limits.

Source: Social Security Administration — SSA’s published amounts for 2027. This page was updated in October 2026 with the 2027 figures, which take effect in January 2027. Your own record is what counts: how these amounts apply to you depends on your situation, and only Social Security has your official record.

The Anchors

Numbers that hold steady

Not everything moves with the calendar. These amounts are written into the rules themselves, and both books build on them.

$20 + $65

The exclusions on every paycheck

On SSI, SSA ignores the first $20 of income, then the first $65 of earnings, then counts only half of what is left. That math is why working almost always leaves you with more total money.

$2,000 / $3,000

SSI resource limits

Countable resources are capped at $2,000 for an individual and $3,000 for a couple, counted on the first of the month. Your home and one vehicle do not count.

$100,000

The ABLE disregard

Money in an ABLE account does not count as an SSI resource up to $100,000 — the largest legal savings shelter in the system.
